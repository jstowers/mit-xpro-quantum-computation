## What is a Quantum Algorithm?

Applying a series of gate operations to a set of quantum bits in order to generate quantum states.

Before running an algorithm, we need to check:

1. Are gates operating in the way we expect them to?

2. Are we creating the states we intend to create?

## Characterizing a Quantum State

To understand how to represent a quantum state, we

1. Need to know _how_ to represent a quantum state

2. Need to know what happens when we _measure_ a quantum state


## What is Tomography?

Reconstructing something from many pieces of incomplete data.
