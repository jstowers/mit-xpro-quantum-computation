## Review

To fully _characterize_ a quantum process requires an _exponential_ number of measurements.

## Wanted

A scalable method to verify gates operate as expected without completing a full characterization.

## Clifford Group

The Clifford group is a mathematical group of certain gates.

A gate formed from composite Clifford gates is itself a Clifford gate.
