## Goal

What is a legal map, $E$ for:

  $\rho \rightarrow E(\rho)$

Three criteria:

1. Output density matrix must have a trace of 1.

2. E must be a linear and convex operation

3. Output density matrix must be positive; output must be positive even if map, E, only operates on part of the system.  This is known as __complete positivity__.

### Complete Positivity

A completely positive density matrix must possess _exclusviely_ non-negative eigenvalues.

Look at quantum circuit of this criterion.

Reference part does not have E act on it.


Even if a state is negative, it is still mathematically useful as a _negative_ partial transpose test for entanglement.

The "test" of finding negative eigenvalues in the trace density matrix indicates that the original density matrix, $\rho_{AB}$, represents an entangled state.
