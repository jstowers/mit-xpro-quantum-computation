# Quantum Gate Fidelity

Sunday, October 19, 2025

## Question

How well does an _experimental_ quantum operation, ${E}$, represent a _theoretical_ unitary gate or circuit, ${U}$?

## Three Measures of Gate Fidelity

1. Minimum gate fidelity

2. Entanglement fidelity

3. Average gate fidelity

### 1. Minimum Gate Fidelity

Minimum fidelity between ${U}$ acting on some input $\ket{\psi}$ and ${E}$ acting on the same input.

The failure probability is ${p}$

Can be hard to compute.

### 2. Entanglement Fidelity

$\phi$ is a maximally entangled state.

How much entanglement is left intact by quantum operation ${E}$

Entanglement is the precious resources utilized by quantum computation.

Largely impractical to measure today.

### 3. Average Gate Fidelity

Computing the fidelity between U acting on a state, $\psi$, and E acting on the same state, and averaging evenly over all states.
